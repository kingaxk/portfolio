Look at what I’ve done

On-site observational research

Goal: Monitoring logistics for a mass-scale exam event (8k+ students and employees) powered by a custom-built tablet app and analytical dashboards. Ensuring real-time error handling and implementing UX/UI improvements during a multi-day event.

Effect: Implemented immediate UI functional fixes and added security mechanisms, such as forcing system updates on the user side to ensure data consistency. Pinpointed non-automated processes (e.g. examination room equipment logistics) with high potential for future automation. Developed instruction checklists and supporting materials (e.g. contextual site maps with highlighted user flow and app modules used in each station) to streamline the onboarding of temporary staff. Gathered process-speed data to provide insights for future logistical improvements and resource allocation.

Exam process redesign

Goal: Maping the entire examination experience - from preparation to certificate delivery - for students, teachers and school administration. Addressing key pain points and eliminating inefficiencies for all parties.

Effect: Actionable recomendations targeted at root causes of major problems. For example, reducing wasted exam slots by introducing queuing system for students waiting for freed-up terms and fostering greater sense of responsibility in students for their chosen exam dates.

UX strategy, testing & monitoring

Goal: Increasing conversion by improving the user experience. Analyzing existing data to pinpoint friction and define project KPIs. Shaping new UI strategy and validating it with in-depth interviews and usability tests.

Effect: Data-backed UI changes with tighter change control: single dashboard tracking clear KPIs and key user events. Also, an A/B testing strategy to guide further improvements.

Adoption dashboard

Goal: Providing an interactive, dynamic report to explore product adoption across multiple dimensions - including breakdowns by features, modules, use cases, as well as user segments.

Effect: Better understanding of pricing package fit and upselling effectiveness - valuable input for shaping new pricing strategies.

Calculating supply for exam slots

Goal: Ensuring the school has sufficient number of teachers to meet exam demand, including buffer for reschedules, while accounting for seasonal variation of teachers' duties and subject-specific peaks of students' preferences.

Effect: Hiring strategy and allocation of multi-subject teachers, avoiding both shortages and excessive overstaffing. Combined behavioral data with students’ declarations to generate personalized timetables, with mechanisms for manual adjustments still ensuring balanced teachers workload and protected reserves for reschedules.

Report on mobile app usage during the pandemic

Goal: Analyzing passive mobile data to explore how user behavior and app engagement patterns shifted throughout the COVID-19 pandemic.

Effect: Published article highlighting category-level changes, time trends and adaptation patterns across different user groups.

Boardgames popularity analysis

Goal: Visualizing boardgame design trends based on data from BoardGamesGeek - the most popular global board gaming portal - using its Top 1000 ranking.

Effect: Insights into player preferences and genre dynamics, including the most suitable mechanics for specific game category. This analysis serves as groundwork for future project development.

Employee experience data processing

Goal: Transforming raw survey data from 1000 respondents into an insightful, accessible visualizations. Including NPS calculations and statistical significance verification.

Effect: Clear charts and comparisons that help readers quickly spot significant differences and grasp key trends.

User needs prioritization board

Goal: Aggregating feedback from multiple sources - users and internal teams such as Sales, Marketing and Customer Service - to identify the most common needs, uncover market gaps and benchmark innovative ideas against them.

Effect: Improved strategic clarity and more effective initiative prioritization - driven by a clear single-value indicator and its breakdown into key factors like impact, effort and current module assessment by users.

Personas and ICP for SaaS product

Goal: Segmenting clients and defining personas as well as Ideal Client Profile based on usage patterns, characteristics of the businesses and operating environment.

Effect: Sharper targeting, clearer communication and better product strategy alignment across entire organization - from Sales and Marketing to Product and Design teams.

Menu redesign for marketing automation app

Goal: Simplifying the main menu to make navigation faster and more intuitive.

Effect: Improved user orientation by aligning structure with users’ mental models and language. Reduced cognitive load thanks to applying core usability and information architecture principles. Clearer understanding of the product’s modules and connections by clients, leading to increased adoption of underused features and more efficient usage overall.

New classroom platform in school ecosystem

Goal: Providing platform for extracurricular activities for children with disabilities. Key features: dedicated booking list for each student based on their diagnosis, reminders for participants and for teachers to complete documentation, tools for leaders to verify specialists' logs and manage class substitutions, dashboards for analytics and monitoring.

Effect: New platform fully integrated with the main e-learning system, with enhanced accessibility standards in global Design System. Improved organization of classes, safer documentation flows and an all-in-one space to support students with disabilities.

Short logic games for cross-team integration

Goal: Designing short, engaging activity: logic mini-games mixed with small-talk prompts to strengthen cross-department integration and highlight the value of building on individual strengths - reinforced by a shared motto displayed on the projector and “unlocked” by teams during the activity.

Effect: Participants were assigned to tables using a profiling survey (based on their strengths and team). The format triggered noticeably stronger cross-department interaction and collaboration than standard morning meetings. Teams wanted to keep the habit of using conversation prompts as icebreakers.

Exam operations app

Goal: Designing a QR-code-based scanner flow for students and employees that automatically detects the nearest exam session and provides context-aware information, such as next-exam reminders upon exit. Enabling real-time attendance tracking via dashboards and instant assignment of reserve teachers by the exam coordinator whenever an examiner is missing.

Effect: Faster response to staffing gaps thanks to a clear, live overview of exam room readiness. Reliable, real-time data regarding exam attendance and the exact number of people inside the venue for safety and evacuation purposes, significantly improving overall coordination during state exams.

VR feedback design

Effect: Intuitive user flows and engaging interaction patterns, grounded in user interviews and academic research - ensuring that the experience is engaging and effectively supports development of public speaking skills and builds user confidence, as well as persuasive skill, over time.

Pricing page design

Effect: Improved user understanding of available plans and product roadmap.

Interactive prototype for usability testing

Effect: Ability to quickly test designs without developer implementation - reducing risk and enabling early identification of usability issues. Alignment on user flows and interactions across stakeholders.

AI script improving feedback quality

Goal: Using new technologies and product documentation to automatically classify feedback and enrich it with contextual information - speeding up Design team work.

Dynamic hiring form with PDF generation

Goal: Transforming static spreadsheet into a fully functional form with automated PDF generation and cloud-based data archiving and storage, streamlining the hiring process.

Effect: Faster process due to automatic generation of required documents - eliminating hard, non-intuitive steps. Fewer documentation errors - thanks to dynamic fields and validation, also reducing the need for time-consuming corrections.

Trip enrollment automation

Goal: Generating print-ready PDFs grouped by departure date. Each PDF including a table of submitted data plus accepted declarations and signatures, with dedicated logic for minors (adjusted set of declarations with required guardian consent and signatures).

Effect: One-click printing process for facilitators - from a single folder per trip - speeding up their work and guest check-in.

School statute generator

Goal: Generating school statute based on a survey describing the planned school model, supported by domain knowledge from an institution running a program for newly established schools.

Effect: Faster creation of consistent, compliant drafts with fewer errors and manual edits. Making it easier to move from a concept to a ready statute aligned with the school’s needs.

Class schedule generator

Goal: Reducing manual effort in scheduling 300+ class groups held several times a week. Taking into account students' preferred time slots (based on historical data) while avoiding slots collisions within a given student profile, so that students have the widest possible choice and can enroll in multiple classes. Respecting teachers' preferences regarding how their time is distributed across specific class types or student profiles, while maximizing working time utilization.

Effect: Maximized use of teacher availability. Greater diversity of available time slots for students within a given profile. A ready-to-upload schedule for the classes platform, significantly speeding up the class-creation process.
