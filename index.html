About Me

I'm Kinga Kornacka – a Service Designer and Data Strategist with a background in UX research and cognitive psychology. I work at the intersection of technology, design, and business – helping teams uncover insights, automate decisions, and build products that make sense.

My experience spans SaaS platforms, customer journey optimization, and predictive analytics. I’m passionate about turning raw data into usable knowledge – from dashboards to ML-driven reports. Think black box, but with an open door.

When I’m not working with data or mapping services, I’m learning new tools, mentoring junior designers, or thinking about ethical design and parent-friendly workplaces.
